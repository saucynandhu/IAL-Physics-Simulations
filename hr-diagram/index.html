Understanding the HR Diagram

The HR diagram plots stars by their temperature and luminosity, revealing patterns in stellar evolution.

L = 4πR²σT⁴

Stefan-Boltzmann Law: Luminosity depends on radius and temperature.

L ∝ M³·⁵

Mass-Luminosity Relation for main sequence stars.

λₘₐₓ = b/T

Wien's Law: Peak wavelength inversely proportional to temperature. Hot stars appear blue, cool stars appear red.
