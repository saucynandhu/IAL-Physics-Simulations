Radioactive Decay Theory

Radioactive decay is a random process at the level of single atoms, but predictable statistically for large samples. The probability of decay per unit time is constant for a given isotope.

N = N₀e^(-λt)

Number of undecayed nuclei at time t, where N₀ is initial number and λ is decay constant.

A = λN = A₀e^(-λt)

Activity (decay rate) at time t, where A₀ is initial activity.

t½ = ln(2)/λ

Half-life - time for half the nuclei to decay.

λ = ln(2)/t½

Decay constant in terms of half-life.
