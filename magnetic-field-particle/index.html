Cloud Chamber - Charged Particles in Magnetic Field

Magnetic Fields & Particle Physics

A cloud chamber visualizes charged particles moving through a uniform magnetic field. The radius of curvature depends on the particle's momentum, charge, and the field strength - fundamental concepts in particle physics.

r = p/BQ = mv/BQ

Radius of curvature: larger momentum or smaller charge → larger radius

ω = BQ/m

Cyclotron frequency: depends on charge-to-mass ratio (Q/m)

F = QvB sin(θ)

Lorentz force: perpendicular to both velocity and magnetic field

p = mv

Momentum: heavier particles or faster particles have larger momentum

T = 2πm/BQ

Period: independent of velocity - key insight for particle accelerators
