Simple Harmonic Motion Theory

Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) is a type of periodic motion where the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement and acts in the opposite direction to the displacement.

x = A cos(ωt + φ)

Displacement equation, where A is amplitude, ω is angular frequency, t is time, and φ is phase constant.

F = -kx

Hooke's Law - restoring force, where k is spring constant and x is displacement.

T = 2π√(m/k)

Period of oscillation for mass-spring system, where m is mass and k is spring constant.

T = 2π√(L/g)

Period of oscillation for simple pendulum, where L is length and g is gravitational acceleration.

v = ±ω√(A² - x²)

Velocity as a function of displacement.

a = -ω²x

Acceleration is always proportional to displacement and opposite in direction.
