Stokes Law Theory

Basic Principles

Stokes Law describes the force of viscosity on a sphere moving through a fluid. The drag force is given by:

F = 6πηrv

Where:

  • F is the drag force (in Newtons)
  • η (eta) is the dynamic viscosity (in Pa·s)
  • r is the radius of the sphere (in meters)
  • v is the velocity of the sphere (in m/s)

Terminal Velocity

When a sphere falls through a fluid, it reaches terminal velocity when the drag force equals the gravitational force:

v_t = (2r²g(ρ_s - ρ_f))/(9η)

Where:

  • v_t is the terminal velocity
  • ρ_s is the density of the sphere
  • ρ_f is the density of the fluid
  • g is acceleration due to gravity (9.81 m/s²)

Important Concepts

  • Viscosity:
    • Measure of fluid's resistance to flow
    • Higher viscosity = more resistance
    • Units: Pascal-seconds (Pa·s)
  • Terminal Velocity:
    • Constant velocity reached when forces balance
    • Depends on sphere size and fluid properties
    • Larger spheres fall faster
  • Applications:
    • Sedimentation analysis
    • Viscosity measurement
    • Particle size determination
    • Industrial processes
