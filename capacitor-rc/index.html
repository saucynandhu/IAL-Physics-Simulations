RC Circuit Theory

An RC circuit consists of a resistor (R) and capacitor (C) connected in series. When voltage is applied, the capacitor charges through the resistor, and when removed, it discharges through the resistor.

Q = Q₀(1 - e^(-t/RC))

Charge on capacitor during charging, where Q₀ is maximum charge.

Q = Q₀e^(-t/RC)

Charge on capacitor during discharging.

V = V₀(1 - e^(-t/RC))

Voltage across capacitor during charging.

I = (V₀/R)e^(-t/RC)

Current through circuit during charging.

τ = RC

Time constant - time to reach 63.2% of final value.
